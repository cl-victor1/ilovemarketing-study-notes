# I Love Marketing: My Study Notes

I keep coming back to the [I Love Marketing](https://ilovemarketing.com/podcasts/) podcast, where
Joe Polish and Dean Jackson talk through direct response marketing, lead generation, referrals, and
the thinking behind a business that is fun to run. The archive has more than 500 episodes, and I
wanted a way to work through all of it and still find an idea again months later.

So I went through the whole show, oldest episode first. For each episode I wrote a short note in my
own words: who was on the show, what the conversation was about, and the three ideas I want to
remember. I worked from transcripts, and an artificial intelligence assistant helped me draft and organize the notes.
There are no quotes from the show in here. If a note catches your interest, listen to the episode
itself; every note links to the original audio.

## How to use these notes

- Pick your language and a range of episodes in the table below.
- Each file covers 100 episodes, in the order they were published.
- Search a file for a guest or a topic, for example referrals or email marketing, to jump to it.
- Treat a note as a pointer to the episode, not a replacement for it. The details, stories, and
  examples live in the conversation.

I also translated the notes into eight more languages with machine translation, so the wording in
those versions can be rough in places.
